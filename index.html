01 — THE PROBLEM

Search used to give ten links. AI gives one answer.

Buyers who once typed a search and worked down a page of results now ask a question and get a short recommendation naming two or three companies. There is no page two. If your name is not in the answer, you were never in the running — and nothing in your analytics will tell you it happened.

The models build their picture of you from your website, directories, reviews, press and LinkedIn. Where that evidence is thin, inconsistent or out of date, they either skip you or describe you wrong. Both are fixable, but only once you know which is happening.

02 — WHAT WE AUDIT

What you get

Fixed scope, fixed fee, and a report written so your own team or your web agency can act on it without me.

  • The questions your customers ask

    We build a list of the real buying questions for your sector, then run every one across ChatGPT, Gemini and Google's AI answers — recording who gets named, in what order, with dates and screenshots.

  • Who is being named instead of you

  • What the models can read on your site

    Page structure, headings, structured data, crawlability, and whether your services, locations and credentials are stated in a form a machine can extract.

  • Whether your facts agree with each other

    Your name, address, services, accreditations and claims as they appear on your site, Google Business, directories, LinkedIn and press. Contradictions are one of the most common reasons a model leaves a company out.

  • The evidence trail behind you

    Reviews, mentions, citations and third-party coverage — the material the models actually draw on when deciding who to recommend.

  • Every market you sell into

    The same tests in each market's own language, not just English.

  • What to change, in order

    A prioritised list separating quick fixes from real technical work, sequenced over 30, 60 and 90 days — then a retest so you can see what moved.
